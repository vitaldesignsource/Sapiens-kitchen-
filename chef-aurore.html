AURORE DE BEAUDUY · CHEF & OWNER

FROM FRANCE TO SCOTTSDALE

A calling.
And a lifetime of craft.

Aurore de Beauduy began her culinary training in France as a teenager. Studies at Cordon Bleu and Ferrandi were followed by work in Paris, including Potel & Chabot and the kitchens of La Tour d’Argent and Les Ambassadeurs.

In 1988, her career took her to the United States. Chicago brought hotel kitchens, country clubs, and a role leading Boeing’s corporate dining. Arizona became home in 2004, where she helped establish the fine-dining restaurant at Silverleaf.

In 2006, she and her husband opened Vogue Bistro, bringing classic French technique to a contemporary American setting. A deepening interest in fresh produce and local farms later led to a new direction: Sapiens Paleo Kitchen.

Today, that idea brings French culinary craft and Paleo principles to the same table—thoughtfully made food, generous flavor, and a warm Scottsdale welcome.
