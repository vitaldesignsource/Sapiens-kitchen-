Sapiens Paleo Kitchen is a French restaurant BYOB owned by Chef Aurore de Beauduy, serving French Cuisine with Paleo nutrition principles. Sapiens Paleo Kitchen is the first and only restaurant in Arizona offering a 100% Paleo/Gluten-Free/Dairy-Free/Soy-Free/Seed Oil-Free menu. We are proud to be the place where you can order with your eyes closed and enjoy a delicious meal carefully chosen to include the ingredients that contribute to your well-being and prepared with the best culinary practices. We believe in great service as much as we believe that food is medicine. Sapiens Paleo Kitchen also offers organic Meal Prep, locally sourced, ready-to-eat meals prepared in Paleo, KETO, Anti-inflammatory (AIP) and Vegan varieties. Join us to discover the best of French cooking delivering the promise of optimal health.

THE PEOPLE & THE PHILOSOPHY

A French heart.
A thoughtful kitchen.

Chef and owner Aurore de Beauduy and general manager/owner Roman Yasinsky developed Sapiens around French culinary technique and Paleo principles. The restaurant’s published philosophy emphasizes unprocessed, locally sourced produce, grass-fed meats, wild-caught fish, seafood, pasture-raised eggs, quality fats, vegetables, nuts, seeds and fruits.

Its ingredient philosophy discusses avoiding grains, dairy, processed sugar, beans and legumes, trans fats and industrial oils, GMO foods, corn and soy. The team supports discussions of Keto, Vegan, Whole30, celiac/gluten-free and AIP requirements; tell the kitchen about any allergies.

Sapiens is an independently owned restaurant for casual dinners, business meals and intimate evenings, with a sociable setting and attentive service. It is licensed for BYOB service; the published corkage fee is $15.

OUR LOCAL SUPPLIER

A connection
to the farm.

The restaurant’s supplier feature introduces RhibaFarms in San Tan Valley, Arizona, and owner Mark Rhine. It describes a local, sustainable, certified-organic farm offering ripe seasonal produce, including unusual varieties and a wide selection of vegetables.

This interest in ingredients extends across dinner, individually packaged weekly meal prep, the Paleo bakery, Gourmet Shop selections and full-service catering.
