The published menu is dated February 4, 2025. Standard orders start at $200 before tax and fees, with 48 hours’ notice. Most entrées require 6 of each selection; displays, stations, appetizers, desserts and soups have their own minimums below.

The PDF describes its core Paleo preparations as free of gluten, grains, corn, sugar, dairy, beans, soy, peanuts, processed additives, GMO products, trans fats, seed oils and artificial ingredients, with Keto, Whole30 and AIP support. It also lists event selections and optional extras containing cheese and other dairy. Confirm a menu that meets your dietary needs with the catering team.
