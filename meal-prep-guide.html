FROM YOUR FRIDGE TO YOUR TABLE

A little
preparation.

Published reheating guidance: microwave for 1 minute, or use a 350°F oven for 4 minutes. Follow any instructions included with the individual dish; the extra-protein menu notes that some proteins arrive lightly seared or medium rare and require longer cooking for a greater degree of doneness.

Meals come in recyclable, microwaveable containers. The ordering policy calls for refrigeration and consumption within 7 days. Provide delivery instructions and an approved recipient or location if you will not be there to accept the order.

CHOOSE YOUR WAY

À la carte,
or a weekly ritual.

À la carte orders let you choose from the Paleo and Keto menus. Add proteins, sides, soups, salads, sauces, spreads, bread or desserts. The published minimum is 5 meals or $69.75. One Sapiens Paleo Bread may be ordered per order.

Subscriptions are automatically renewed every week until canceled. Register or sign in, select a weekly meal count, add any recurring extras and complete checkout. Chef Aurore selects complete meals around your preferences and the available produce and proteins.

Enter Keto, Vegan, AIP, McDougall, allergy or other custom requirements in the Dietary Restrictions field at checkout. AIP vegetable substitutions are available. Discuss specific needs with the team before you order.

INGREDIENTS & PLANNING

A considered
kitchen.

The published meal-prep policy excludes gluten/grains, dairy, sugar, soy, corn, beans, trans fats, GMO products, industrial vegetable oils such as canola, peanut and sunflower, processed ingredients and food additives. Individual dishes may contain other nuts or seeds: read each description and tell us about allergies.

The meal-prep overview asks for orders by Sunday at 11 pm. The formal ordering terms state Sunday at midnight; ordering by the earlier time avoids this discrepancy. Late orders normally move to the next delivery cycle unless the team confirms last-minute availability.

Published menus rotate every other Sunday after midnight. For a custom plan or an order question: (480) 771-5124 · mealprep@sapienskitchen.com.
