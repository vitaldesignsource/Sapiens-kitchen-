START, MANAGE & RENEW

Your weekly plan.
Your preferences.

Sign in or register in My Account, select 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 or 20 meals per week, and complete checkout. Add recurring extras if desired and adjust quantities in the cart.

Keto, Vegan, AIP and other custom requirements belong in the Dietary Restrictions field at checkout. Include allergies and delivery instructions. Chef-selected meals are prepared each week automatically around your requirements.

A subscription renews weekly until canceled. The published process for changing it is to cancel the existing subscription in My Account and create a new one. The ordering policy asks for cancellation and changes by the preceding Sunday at midnight; later changes may incur the next charge.

Delivery is scheduled weekly on Wednesday to the address supplied, or you may choose pickup. A published $12.99 delivery fee is added to each weekly subscription when delivery is selected. Confirm the final time window at checkout.
