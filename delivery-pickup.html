À VOTRE PORTE

Your week,
delivered.

Meal-prep orders are delivered on Wednesdays. Pickup is available at the restaurant on Wednesday and Thursday. Check the current pickup and delivery window at checkout; published pages show different time windows.

Order before the Sunday cutoff for the following week. The meal-prep page asks for orders by 11 pm. Contact the team for last-minute availability.

The published minimum is five meals or $69.75. The ordering terms list a $12.99 delivery fee. Your final options and total appear during checkout.

Delivery serves selected ZIP codes in Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Fountain Hills, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Glendale, and Peoria. Confirm your exact address with the team.

For Gourmet Shop or catering orders, arrange delivery or pickup directly with the restaurant. For questions, contact (480) 771-5124 or mealprep@sapienskitchen.com.

THE SCHEDULE, IN FULL

Your delivery.
Your arrangements.

The meal-prep overview lists Wednesday delivery noon–8 pm and Wednesday–Thursday pickup 4:30–10 pm. The à-la-carte menu footers list delivery 11 am–8 pm and the same pickup window.

The subscription page lists Wednesday delivery 2–9 pm and Wednesday–Thursday pickup 4–9 pm. The formal ordering policy lists Wednesday delivery 11 am–9 pm and Wednesday–Thursday pickup 4–9 pm. Because the published windows differ, confirm the final time for your order with the team.

A $12.99 meal-prep delivery charge is published in the ordering terms. Select delivery or pickup in the cart. If the address is outside the coverage area, the policy provides for pickup at Sapiens Kitchen; call in advance to ask about a mutually arranged highway or crossroads pickup location.

Provide delivery instructions at checkout. The policy allows delivery to a trusted party when arranged by the customer. A missed delivery is returned to the restaurant for pickup; its policy specifies Wednesday–Thursday 4–9 pm and possible disposal without refund if still uncollected Friday at 9 pm.
